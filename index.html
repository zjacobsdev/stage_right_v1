Permissions & Indicators

This app requires access to your camera and microphone to detect hand gestures, monitor lighting/view, and provide live transcription.

  • When the browser prompts, click Allow for camera and microphone access. If you deny, use the site lock icon in the address bar to reopen permissions.
  • If camera or mic don't appear to work, try refreshing the page after granting permissions and ensure no other app is using the devices.
  • For best results, sit facing the camera with steady lighting and hold gestures steadily near the camera.
What the indicators mean
  • View Status: shows whether your face is detected and roughly how much of the camera view you occupy — Good, OK, or Poor.
  • Lighting: estimates scene brightness — Good, OK, or Poor. Improve lighting if it's Poor.
  • Mic: shows microphone availability and whether the app is listening or detecting speech. A level percentage appears while you speak.

As you speak, keywords from the selected notes will be highlighted in the notes area so you can track how well you're hitting key points.

Hand gesture commands (hold to trigger)

Use hand gestures near the camera. Hold the gesture for about 2 seconds to trigger the action.

  • Open palm — Start session
  • Fist — Stop session
  • Thumbs up — Save session
  • Point (index) — Hold to enter scroll mode; move hand up/down to scroll notes

Tip: sit facing the camera with good lighting. If gestures don't trigger, try moving slightly closer and hold the pose steady.
